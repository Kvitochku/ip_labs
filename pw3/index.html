Fun Trivia Facts

Did you know? The longest game of Monopoly lasted 70 days. That is over two months of rolling dice, buying properties, and going to jail!

The word "quiz" was allegedly invented in 1791 in Dublin. A theater manager bet he could introduce a new word into the English language within 48 hours.

A group of flamingos is called a "flamboyance." These elegant pink birds certainly live up to their collective name!
